## Good Morning, Class!|Selamat Pagi, Anak-anak!|The first minutes of an English lesson.
T: Good morning, class!|Selamat pagi, anak-anak!
Class: Good morning, Miss!|Selamat pagi, Bu!
T: How are you today?|Apa kabar hari ini?
Class: We're fine, thank you. And you?|Kami baik, terima kasih. Ibu bagaimana?
T: I'm very well, thank you. Please sit down.|Ibu baik sekali, terima kasih. Silakan duduk.
T: What day is it today?|Hari apa sekarang?
Dewi: It's Monday, Miss.|Hari Senin, Bu.
T: That's right. And what's the weather like?|Betul. Bagaimana cuacanya?
Budi: It's sunny!|Cerah!
T: Good. Are you ready to learn?|Bagus. Siap belajar?
Class: Yes, Miss!|Siap, Bu!
## Who Is Absent?|Siapa yang Tidak Hadir?|Taking the register.
T: Let me check the attendance. Putu?|Ibu absen dulu, ya. Putu?
Putu: Present, Miss!|Hadir, Bu!
T: Komang?|Komang?
Class: He's absent, Miss.|Dia tidak masuk, Bu.
T: Oh. Why is he absent? Is he sick?|Oh. Kenapa dia tidak masuk? Apakah dia sakit?
Putu: Yes, Miss. He has a fever.|Ya, Bu. Dia demam.
T: Oh dear. I hope he gets well soon.|Aduh. Semoga dia cepat sembuh.
T: Siti?|Siti?
Siti: Here, Miss!|Ada, Bu!
T: Good. Everybody else is here. Thank you.|Bagus. Yang lain hadir semua. Terima kasih.
## Sorry, I'm Late|Maaf, Saya Terlambat|A student arrives late.
Rizky: Excuse me, Miss. May I come in?|Permisi, Bu. Boleh saya masuk?
T: Yes, come in, Rizky. You're late. What happened?|Ya, masuk, Rizky. Kamu terlambat. Ada apa?
Rizky: Sorry, Miss. The traffic was very bad.|Maaf, Bu. Jalannya macet sekali.
T: Okay. Please try to come early tomorrow.|Baik. Besok usahakan datang lebih awal, ya.
Rizky: Yes, Miss. Sorry.|Ya, Bu. Maaf.
T: Go to your seat and open your book to page twelve.|Ke tempat dudukmu dan buka bukumu halaman dua belas.
Rizky: Thank you, Miss.|Terima kasih, Bu.
## Open Your Books|Buka Buku Kalian|Starting an activity from the textbook.
T: Now, take out your English books.|Sekarang, keluarkan buku bahasa Inggris kalian.
T: Open your books to page twenty, please.|Buka buku halaman dua puluh, ya.
Dewi: What page, Miss?|Halaman berapa, Bu?
T: Page twenty. Two, zero.|Halaman dua puluh. Dua, nol.
T: Look at the picture. What can you see?|Lihat gambarnya. Apa yang bisa kalian lihat?
Budi: I can see a cat!|Saya melihat seekor kucing!
Siti: And three birds!|Dan tiga burung!
T: Very good! What colour is the cat?|Bagus sekali! Kucingnya warna apa?
Class: It's orange!|Oranye!
T: Excellent! Now listen and repeat.|Hebat! Sekarang dengarkan dan ulangi.
## Checking Homework|Memeriksa PR|Collecting and checking homework.
T: Did you do your homework?|Apakah kalian mengerjakan PR?
Class: Yes, Miss!|Sudah, Bu!
T: Great. Please put your homework on my desk.|Bagus. Tolong taruh PR kalian di meja Ibu.
Putu: Miss, I forgot my homework at home.|Bu, PR saya ketinggalan di rumah.
T: Oh no. Please bring it tomorrow, okay?|Waduh. Tolong bawa besok, ya?
Putu: Okay, Miss. Sorry.|Baik, Bu. Maaf.
T: Let's check number one together. What's the answer?|Ayo periksa nomor satu bersama. Apa jawabannya?
Siti: The answer is "B", Miss.|Jawabannya "B", Bu.
T: That's right. Well done, Siti!|Betul. Bagus sekali, Siti!
## Pair Work|Kerja Berpasangan|Setting up a speaking activity.
T: Now, work in pairs. Turn to your partner.|Sekarang, kerjakan berpasangan. Hadap ke pasanganmu.
T: Student A, ask "What's your favourite food?"|Siswa A, tanyakan "What's your favourite food?"
T: Student B, answer. Then swap.|Siswa B, jawab. Lalu bertukar.
Budi: Miss, I don't have a partner.|Bu, saya tidak punya pasangan.
T: You can work with Dewi and Siti. Make a group of three.|Kamu boleh bergabung dengan Dewi dan Siti. Buat kelompok bertiga.
Budi: Okay, thank you, Miss.|Baik, terima kasih, Bu.
T: You have five minutes. Ready? Start!|Waktunya lima menit. Siap? Mulai!
T: Time's up! Who wants to show us?|Waktu habis! Siapa yang mau menunjukkan?
Dewi: Me, Miss!|Saya, Bu!
## May I Go to the Toilet?|Boleh Saya ke Toilet?|Handling a common request.
Komang: Excuse me, Miss.|Permisi, Bu.
T: Yes, Komang?|Ya, Komang?
Komang: May I go to the toilet, please?|Boleh saya ke toilet?
T: Yes, you may. But please be quick.|Ya, boleh. Tapi cepat, ya.
Komang: Thank you, Miss.|Terima kasih, Bu.
T: And remember to wash your hands.|Dan ingat cuci tangan.
## I Don't Feel Well|Saya Kurang Enak Badan|A student feels sick.
T: Siti, are you okay? You look tired.|Siti, kamu baik-baik saja? Kamu kelihatan lelah.
Siti: I don't feel well, Miss.|Saya kurang enak badan, Bu.
T: What's wrong?|Kenapa?
Siti: I have a headache.|Kepala saya sakit.
T: Oh, I'm sorry. Do you want to go to the school nurse?|Oh, kasihan. Kamu mau ke UKS?
Siti: Yes, please.|Iya, Bu.
T: Dewi, can you take Siti to the school nurse, please?|Dewi, bisa antar Siti ke UKS?
Dewi: Yes, Miss.|Bisa, Bu.
T: Thank you, Dewi. Get well soon, Siti.|Terima kasih, Dewi. Cepat sembuh, Siti.
## Let's Play "Simon Says"|Ayo Main "Simon Says"|A Total Physical Response (TPR) game.
T: Let's play a game! Stand up, everyone.|Ayo bermain! Semua berdiri.
T: Listen carefully. Only move when I say "Simon says".|Dengarkan baik-baik. Bergerak hanya kalau Ibu bilang "Simon says".
T: Simon says, touch your head.|Simon berkata, sentuh kepalamu.
T: Simon says, clap your hands.|Simon berkata, tepuk tanganmu.
T: Jump!|Lompat!
T: Oh, Rizky, you jumped! Simon didn't say. Please sit down.|Oh, Rizky, kamu melompat! Simon tidak bilang. Silakan duduk.
Rizky: Oh no!|Yah!
T: Simon says, touch your nose.|Simon berkata, sentuh hidungmu.
T: Great job, everyone! Now sit down, please.|Kerja bagus, semuanya! Sekarang silakan duduk.
## Reading Aloud|Membaca Nyaring|Praise and gentle correction.
T: Putu, can you read number one, please?|Putu, tolong baca nomor satu.
Putu: "I have tree pencils."|"I have tree pencils."
T: Nice try! Listen: "three". Th-th-three.|Usaha yang bagus! Dengar: "three". Th-th-three.
Putu: Three. "I have three pencils."|Three. "I have three pencils."
T: Excellent! That's right.|Luar biasa! Betul.
T: Everybody, repeat: three pencils.|Semuanya, ulangi: three pencils.
Class: Three pencils!|Three pencils!
T: Very good. Louder, please!|Bagus. Lebih keras lagi!
## How Was Your Weekend?|Bagaimana Akhir Pekanmu?|Small talk on Monday morning.
T: How was your weekend, Dewi?|Bagaimana akhir pekanmu, Dewi?
Dewi: It was great, Miss!|Menyenangkan, Bu!
T: What did you do?|Apa yang kamu lakukan?
Dewi: I went to the beach with my family.|Saya ke pantai bersama keluarga.
T: Wow, nice! Did you swim?|Wah, asyik! Kamu berenang?
Dewi: Yes, I did. And I played with sand.|Iya, Bu. Dan saya bermain pasir.
T: That's lovely. And you, Budi?|Menyenangkan sekali. Kalau kamu, Budi?
Budi: I played football with my friends.|Saya main sepak bola dengan teman-teman.
T: Great! Did you win?|Hebat! Kamu menang?
Budi: Yes, we won!|Ya, kami menang!
## See You Tomorrow!|Sampai Jumpa Besok!|Closing the lesson.
T: Okay, class. It's time to stop.|Baik, anak-anak. Sudah waktunya berhenti.
T: What did we learn today?|Apa yang kita pelajari hari ini?
Class: Animals!|Hewan!
T: Yes! Tell me one animal in English.|Ya! Sebutkan satu hewan dalam bahasa Inggris.
Komang: Elephant!|Gajah!
T: Good! Your homework is on page twenty-five.|Bagus! PR kalian di halaman dua puluh lima.
T: Please tidy up your desks and put the rubbish in the bin.|Tolong rapikan meja dan buang sampah ke tempat sampah.
T: Goodbye, class. See you tomorrow!|Sampai jumpa, anak-anak. Sampai jumpa besok!
Class: Goodbye, Miss! See you tomorrow!|Sampai jumpa, Bu! Sampai jumpa besok!
