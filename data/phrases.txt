## start|🌅|Starting the Lesson|Membuka Pelajaran|Use the same opening every day. After 2 weeks, students will answer automatically — and you will feel confident too.
Good morning, class!|Selamat pagi, anak-anak!
Good morning, everyone. How are you today?|Selamat pagi, semuanya. Apa kabar hari ini?
I'm fine, thank you. And you?|Saya baik, terima kasih. Kalau kamu?
Let's pray before we start.|Mari berdoa sebelum kita mulai.
Please sit down.|Silakan duduk.
Is everybody ready?|Apakah semua sudah siap?
Let's start our lesson.|Mari kita mulai pelajaran kita.
Today we are going to learn about animals.|Hari ini kita akan belajar tentang hewan.
What day is it today?|Hari apa sekarang?
What's the date today?|Tanggal berapa hari ini?
What's the weather like today?|Bagaimana cuaca hari ini?
Do you remember our last lesson?|Apakah kalian ingat pelajaran kita sebelumnya?
## register|📋|Taking the Register|Mengabsen|Teach students to answer “Present!” or “Here!”. It is a 1-minute daily speaking practice.
Let me check the attendance.|Saya periksa kehadiran dulu.
When I call your name, say "Present!"|Kalau nama kalian dipanggil, jawab "Hadir!"
Who is absent today?|Siapa yang tidak hadir hari ini?
Where is Budi today?|Budi di mana hari ini?
Is he sick?|Apakah dia sakit?
Everybody is here. Great!|Semua hadir. Hebat!
You're late. What happened?|Kamu terlambat. Ada apa?
Please don't be late again.|Tolong jangan terlambat lagi.
## instructions|🗣️|Giving Instructions|Memberi Instruksi|Say the instruction, then show it with a gesture (point, open your hands like a book). Gestures help students understand without translation.
Open your books, please.|Silakan buka buku kalian.
Turn to page twenty.|Buka halaman dua puluh.
Close your books.|Tutup buku kalian.
Look at the board.|Lihat ke papan tulis.
Look at the picture.|Lihat gambarnya.
Listen carefully.|Dengarkan baik-baik.
Listen and repeat.|Dengarkan dan ulangi.
Repeat after me.|Ulangi setelah saya.
Read the text aloud.|Bacakan teksnya dengan keras.
Read it silently.|Baca dalam hati.
Write your name at the top.|Tulis nama kalian di bagian atas.
Copy this into your notebook.|Salin ini ke buku tulis kalian.
Take out your pencil.|Keluarkan pensil kalian.
Put your things away.|Simpan barang-barang kalian.
Stand up, please.|Silakan berdiri.
Come to the front, please.|Silakan maju ke depan.
Go back to your seat.|Kembali ke tempat dudukmu.
Raise your hand if you know the answer.|Angkat tangan kalau kamu tahu jawabannya.
Circle the correct answer.|Lingkari jawaban yang benar.
Match the words with the pictures.|Jodohkan kata dengan gambarnya.
Fill in the blanks.|Isi titik-titiknya.
Colour the picture.|Warnai gambarnya.
You have five minutes.|Waktu kalian lima menit.
## questions|🤔|Asking & Checking Understanding|Bertanya & Memeriksa Pemahaman|Do not only ask “Do you understand?”. Ask a check question: “What page?”, “How many minutes?”.
Do you understand?|Apakah kalian mengerti?
Any questions?|Ada pertanyaan?
Is it clear?|Sudah jelas?
What does "happy" mean?|Apa arti "happy"?
How do you say "buku" in English?|Bagaimana mengatakan "buku" dalam bahasa Inggris?
How do you spell it?|Bagaimana mengejanya?
Who can answer this question?|Siapa yang bisa menjawab pertanyaan ini?
Can you tell me the answer?|Bisakah kamu memberi tahu saya jawabannya?
What is this?|Ini apa?
What colour is it?|Warnanya apa?
How many cats can you see?|Berapa kucing yang bisa kamu lihat?
Is it true or false?|Benar atau salah?
Are you finished?|Sudah selesai?
Say it again, please.|Tolong ucapkan lagi.
Louder, please.|Lebih keras, ya.
## praise|🌟|Praise & Encouragement|Pujian & Semangat|Praise the effort, not only the answer. Short praise in English is the easiest classroom English to start with.
Good job!|Kerja bagus!
Well done!|Bagus sekali!
Excellent!|Luar biasa!
Very good!|Sangat bagus!
That's right!|Betul!
Great work, everyone!|Kerja hebat, semuanya!
Nice try!|Usaha yang bagus!
Almost! Try again.|Hampir! Coba lagi.
Don't worry. It's okay.|Jangan khawatir. Tidak apa-apa.
You can do it!|Kamu pasti bisa!
I'm proud of you.|Saya bangga padamu.
Give him a big clap!|Beri dia tepuk tangan yang meriah!
Your writing is very neat.|Tulisanmu sangat rapi.
## behaviour|🤫|Managing the Class|Mengelola Kelas|Calm and short is best: “Eyes on me, please.” Wait. Smile. Say “Thank you.”
Quiet, please.|Tolong tenang.
Be quiet, please.|Harap tenang.
Listen to me, please.|Tolong dengarkan saya.
Eyes on me, please.|Lihat ke saya, ya.
Sit properly, please.|Duduk yang rapi, ya.
Stop talking, please.|Tolong berhenti mengobrol.
Don't run in the classroom.|Jangan berlari di dalam kelas.
Please be careful.|Tolong hati-hati.
Wait for your turn.|Tunggu giliranmu.
One at a time, please.|Satu per satu, ya.
Hands up, please. Don't shout.|Angkat tangan, ya. Jangan berteriak.
Put your phone away, please.|Tolong simpan HP-mu.
Let's count to three. One, two, three!|Ayo hitung sampai tiga. Satu, dua, tiga!
## groups|👥|Pair & Group Work|Kerja Berpasangan & Kelompok|Model the activity first with one student in front of the class, then say “Now you!”.
Work in pairs.|Bekerjalah berpasangan.
Work in groups of four.|Bekerjalah dalam kelompok berempat.
Find a partner.|Cari pasangan.
Turn to your partner.|Hadap ke pasanganmu.
Make a circle.|Buat lingkaran.
Move your chairs, please.|Tolong pindahkan kursi kalian.
Take turns.|Bergantian, ya.
Ask your friend.|Tanya temanmu.
Share with your partner.|Bagikan dengan pasanganmu.
Who wants to go first?|Siapa yang mau duluan?
Group one, please present your work.|Kelompok satu, silakan presentasikan hasilnya.
Go back to your places.|Kembali ke tempat kalian.
## games|🎲|Games & Songs|Permainan & Lagu|Games are the best time for English-only talk. Students do not mind — they want to play!
Let's play a game!|Ayo bermain!
Let's sing a song.|Ayo bernyanyi.
Make two teams.|Bentuk dua tim.
This is team A, and this is team B.|Ini tim A, dan ini tim B.
It's your turn.|Giliranmu.
Ready? Go!|Siap? Mulai!
Time's up!|Waktu habis!
Who's the winner?|Siapa pemenangnya?
Team B wins! Congratulations!|Tim B menang! Selamat!
Simon says, touch your nose.|Simon berkata, sentuh hidungmu.
Let's sing it again, louder!|Ayo nyanyikan lagi, lebih keras!
## help|🩹|Helping Students|Membantu Siswa|Correct gently: repeat the sentence correctly and smile. No need to say “Wrong!”.
What's wrong?|Ada apa?
Are you okay?|Kamu baik-baik saja?
Do you need help?|Perlu bantuan?
Let me help you.|Saya bantu, ya.
Do you feel sick?|Kamu merasa sakit?
You can go to the school nurse.|Kamu boleh ke UKS.
Have you got a pencil?|Kamu punya pensil?
You can borrow my pen.|Kamu boleh pinjam pulpen saya.
Say it in English, please.|Coba katakan dalam bahasa Inggris.
Not "I am agree". We say "I agree".|Bukan "I am agree". Kita bilang "I agree".
Listen again: "three", not "tree".|Dengar lagi: "three", bukan "tree".
## ending|🏁|Ending the Lesson|Menutup Pelajaran|Finish with the same goodbye routine every day, like the opening.
It's time to stop.|Sudah waktunya berhenti.
What did we learn today?|Apa yang kita pelajari hari ini?
Your homework is on page twenty-five.|PR kalian ada di halaman dua puluh lima.
Don't forget your homework.|Jangan lupa PR-nya.
Please tidy up your desks.|Tolong rapikan meja kalian.
Put the rubbish in the bin.|Buang sampah ke tempat sampah.
Let's pray before we go home.|Mari berdoa sebelum pulang.
See you tomorrow!|Sampai jumpa besok!
See you next week!|Sampai jumpa minggu depan!
Goodbye, everyone. Have a nice day!|Sampai jumpa, semuanya. Semoga harimu menyenangkan!
## students|🙋|What Students Say (teach these!)|Ucapan Siswa (ajarkan ini!)|Put these sentences on a classroom poster. Students may only ask in English — you can help them read it.
Good morning, Miss / Sir.|Selamat pagi, Bu / Pak.
Excuse me, Miss.|Permisi, Bu.
May I come in?|Boleh saya masuk?
May I go to the toilet, please?|Boleh saya ke toilet?
Sorry, I'm late.|Maaf, saya terlambat.
I don't understand.|Saya tidak mengerti.
Can you repeat that, please?|Bisa diulangi?
How do you say ... in English?|Bagaimana mengatakan ... dalam bahasa Inggris?
What page, Miss?|Halaman berapa, Bu?
I've finished.|Saya sudah selesai.
I forgot my book.|Saya lupa membawa buku.
Can I borrow your eraser?|Boleh saya pinjam penghapusmu?
It's my turn!|Giliran saya!
I don't feel well.|Saya merasa kurang enak badan.
Thank you, Miss. Goodbye!|Terima kasih, Bu. Sampai jumpa!
