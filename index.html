6 tips for teaching in English with confidence

You do not need perfect English. You need routine English.

  1. Short sentences + gestures

    “Open your books.” + show an open book with your hands.

    Kalimat pendek + gerakan tangan.

  2. Same words, every day

    Repeat the same opening, register and goodbye. Routines build confidence fast.

    Ulangi ungkapan yang sama setiap hari.

  3. English first, Indonesian if needed

    Say it in English, wait, then translate only if students are lost. Slowly use less Indonesian.

    Bahasa Inggris dulu, terjemahkan bila perlu.

  4. Mistakes are okay

    Your students learn from your effort. A smile and “Let's try again!” is perfect teaching.

    Salah itu wajar — teruslah mencoba.

  5. Teach students their phrases too

    “May I go to the toilet?”, “I don't understand.” Put them on a wall poster. See What Students Say

    Ajarkan juga ungkapan untuk siswa.

  6. Practise out loud

    Say every sentence aloud, not only in your head. Your mouth needs practice too!

    Latih dengan suara keras, bukan dalam hati.
